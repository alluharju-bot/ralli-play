# Scenery models

Eight nature models adapted from Kenney Nature Kit (Kenney), CC0 1.0:
https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original models: tree_oak, tree_thin, tree_pineTallA_detailed,
plant_bushDetailed, plant_bushLargeTriangle, rock_smallA, rock_largeA,
rock_smallFlatA. Scale/palette adapted for The Greatest Racing Game.

Stored under replaceable game IDs; the vendor files are not a runtime dependency.
Other scenery prototypes and vehicle geometry are game-specific.
